Catena-X strives to create an
ecosystem for the development operation
and collaborative use of solutions
to solve the toughest industry problems
across the entire automotive value chain.
Joining the Catena-X ecosystem
as adopter or solution provider
can look quite overwhelming
and may feel complex.
That's why
we want to make access as easy
as possible and help
you get started quickly to realize
the full potential
of the Catena-X ecosystem.
Adopters mainly connect to the Catena-X
data space in a do it yourself way
to provide or consume data.
Solution providers
design and create
interoperable solutions,
profit from development resources
and accelerate their development
using open source.
And for example, our Kits
and now let's head over to Tractus-X,
the home of our Kits.
The Eclipse Tractus-X project
is the official open source project
in the Catena-X ecosystem under
the umbrella of the Eclipse Foundation.
The Eclipse Foundation is a not
for profit corporation that is supported
by over
320 members and represents
the world's largest sponsored collection
of open source projects and developers.
By using an open
and well-established development process,
we enable organizations
and their developers to collaborate
on the design and development of kits.
Kits are released
under the Apache 2.0 license,
so any organization can use,
modify and distribute them
in any environment.
In addition to our kits,
you will also find a forum
for our developer community,
development and release guidelines,
as well as an overview of our releases
on the Tractus-X website.
The Tractus-X project is structured
along the four domains that represent
use cases in Catena-X.
First there is the shared network domain
that provides the basis
and functionality
for the business domains.
It's the foundation to build upon.
Then we have the business domain
PLM and quality.
Resiliency
and sustainability.
Each addressing
a specific set of industry challenges
that Catena-X solves
with the help of Kits.
Each of the domains provide at least
one specific page with a well-defined
toolbox of artifacts
that support the two main stakeholders.
Solution providers, for example,
can combine
multiple kits and use API specifications,
as well as semantic models to develop new
interoperable applications and services.
Adopters, in turn,
can use the kits to build and connect
their existing in-house
infrastructure to the Catena-X
data space.
By adopting reference implementations
and following the quick setup guides.
Join us in building the future
of the automotive industry together.
Thank you for your interest
in the Catena-X Community and Project.
